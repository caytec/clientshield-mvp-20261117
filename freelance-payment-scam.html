Freelance Payment Scam Warning Signs

Reviewed by ClientShield editorial team · Last updated: 2026-11-17

A client should not require you to pay an activation fee, buy supplies from a specified seller, or return part of an overpayment. Gift cards and cryptocurrency can also make recovery difficult.

Safer payment habits

Agree on currency, rate, milestones, due dates, and a protected payment channel before beginning. Do not treat a deposited check as cleared funds until your bank confirms it.

When to pause

Pause if the payment method is unusual, the client creates urgency, or the payment process requires you to send money first. Verify independently instead of relying only on contact details in the message.
