What to Do When a Client Asks for Free Work

Reviewed by ClientShield editorial team · Last updated: 2026-11-17

A short, clearly bounded sample is different from a full deliverable disguised as a test. Ask how long the test should take, who may use it, and whether it is paid.

A professional boundary

You can offer a small portfolio example, propose a paid test, or explain that production work begins after a written agreement. Avoid accusations; simply state your terms.

Questions to clarify

Ask what the client will evaluate, whether the sample will be used commercially, how many revisions are expected, and when payment is due. Vague answers can indicate scope risk even when they are not proof of fraud.
