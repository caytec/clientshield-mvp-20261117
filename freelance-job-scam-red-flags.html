Freelance Job Scam Red Flags

Reviewed by ClientShield editorial team · Last updated: 2026-11-17

Common warning signs include upfront fees, gift cards, cryptocurrency requests, fake checks, guaranteed income, recruitment requirements, and urgent pressure.

Scope and access warnings

Unbounded free tests, vague deliverables, unclear payment dates, suspicious downloads, and requests for passwords deserve a pause. Ask for written terms and verify the client through a separate official channel.

What to do next

Do not send money or sensitive information while you investigate. Save the job details, ask specific questions, and keep the conversation in the platform's approved channel when possible.
