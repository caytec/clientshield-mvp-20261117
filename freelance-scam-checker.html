Freelance Scam Checker

Reviewed by ClientShield editorial team · Last updated: 2026-11-17

Paste a freelance lead into the ClientShield analyzer to screen for upfront fees, suspicious payment methods, free-work requests, urgency, and credential requests.

Use the result carefully

A low score is not proof of safety. Confirm the client's identity, written scope, payment schedule, and communication channel independently. Never send passwords, one-time codes, or bank details.

What the checker looks for

The rules-first report looks for payment pressure, recruitment language, vague deliverables, suspicious downloads, and requests to move payment away from a protected platform. It explains detected evidence rather than making a definitive accusation.
