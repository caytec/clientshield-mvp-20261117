Policy

Privacy

Last updated: 2026-11-17

ClientShield is a no-sign-up educational screening tool. Do not submit passwords, identity documents, bank details, private keys, or confidential documents.

What we process

The message text, optional website text, platform, and selected options are sent to the screening endpoint to produce a result. The site does not fetch, crawl, validate, or visit the website value. It is transmitted as text only.

AI provider processing

If an AI provider is configured, the submitted context needed for analysis may be sent to that provider under its terms. The deterministic screening fallback remains available when no provider is configured or a provider fails.

What we do not do

We do not intentionally publish submitted messages, use raw messages for analytics, or include original text in downloaded share cards. Avoid entering information you are not comfortable sending to a hosted service.

Logs and retention

Serverless infrastructure, hosting providers, and AI providers may create operational logs. The application does not intentionally store raw submissions in an application database. We do not promise absolute deletion or confidentiality.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the site operator through the approved project support channel before production launch. A monitored public contact address should be added before the site is used for production submissions.
