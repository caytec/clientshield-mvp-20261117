Is This Upwork Client a Scam?

Reviewed by ClientShield editorial team · Last updated: 2026-11-17

No checker can prove that a client is legitimate from a message alone. Look for requests to move payment outside the platform, pay an activation fee, buy equipment, or provide account access.

Before accepting

Review the client's history, keep communication and payment within platform rules, clarify milestones, and avoid downloading unexpected files. Ask who owns the work and when each payment is due.

Questions to ask

Ask for the legal client name, written deliverables, payment schedule, revision limits, and an official way to verify the business. A client who refuses ordinary documentation deserves a pause.
